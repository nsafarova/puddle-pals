How it works

clip it, unfold it, stay dry

1

clip it on

Hang your pal from a bag zipper, tote strap or your keys. It is small and light, so it goes everywhere you do.

2

unfold when it rains

When sudden rain hits, unfold the charm into a lightweight poncho with a hood.

3

keep your style dry

The hood keeps your hair dry and the poncho covers your clothes. Fold it back into your pal when the rain stops.

Questions

good to know

What is inside each pal?

Each pal is a soft plush zip pouch with a metal clip. Inside is a lightweight, waterproof hooded poncho folded small. Unzip, unfold, and fold it back into your pal when the rain stops.

How are pals packaged, and can I give one as a gift?

Every pal comes in its own individual package, so each one is ready to give or keep. The trio and the full set arrive together in one gift box. To send a gift, mark your order as a gift at checkout, add a note, and choose gift packaging for a small extra fee.

How much does it cost?

A single pal is $16. The trio, any three pals in one box, is $42. The full set of all five pals is $64. Gift packaging is $3 per order. Prices are early launch pricing.

How long does shipping take?

Orders ship from Houston, Texas within 3 to 5 business days. Standard delivery in the continental United States then takes about 3 to 5 days, and you get tracking by email.

Are other states coming?

Texas first. We plan to add pals for more states, each with its own local characters.
